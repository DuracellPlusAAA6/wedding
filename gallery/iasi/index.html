Iași — Our Wedding City

Welcome to Iași, the city where our story begins. Here is what awaits you when you visit.

Photos via Wikimedia Commons (free licenses): Palace of Culture · Golia Monastery · Botanical Garden · Union Square · Metropolitan Cathedral · Copou

What to Explore

  • Palace of Culture — the iconic landmark of Iași.
  • Golia Monastery — centuries of history in the city center.
  • Botanical Garden — a green escape on the city's edge.
  • City center — pedestrian-friendly streets, cafés, and terraces.

Good to Know

  • Iași International Airport (IAS) is 15 minutes from the city center.
  • September weather is mild: 15–25°C. Pack light layers.
  • The center is walkable; Bolt and Uber cover longer rides.
