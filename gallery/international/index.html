International Package

Make a weekend of it — or a whole week. Photos coming soon; here is what each package includes.

Weekend Package — 150€ per person

  • Hotel nights Thursday, Friday, and Saturday at Hotel Traian.
  • Transfers from/to Iași Airport, church, and venue.
  • Welcome drinks on Thursday at Berăria H.
  • Wedding day on Friday — ceremony and reception.
  • Guided tour of Iași on Saturday.
  • Farewell brunch on Sunday at Grădina Veche.

Week Package — 500€ per person

  • 4 nights, Sunday to Thursday, at Hotel Bella Brașov.
  • Transport between destinations, breakfast and lunch daily.
  • Brașov and Bran Castle (Dracula's Castle).
  • UNESCO-listed Sighișoara Citadel and Peleș Castle in Sinaia.
  • Hiking in Piatra Craiului National Park and the Transfăgărășan Highway.
  • Return to Iași by 18:00 on Thursday.
