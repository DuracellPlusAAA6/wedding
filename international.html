Travel Guide

Flights to Iași

Iași International Airport (IAS) is well-connected to major European cities. Here are some options:

  • From Portugal: Direct flights from Lisbon (LIS) or Porto (OPO) via TAP Air Portugal or Wizz Air.
  • From France: Direct flights from Paris (CDG/BVA) via Air France or Wizz Air.
  • From Other European Cities: Connecting flights via Bucharest (OTP) or Budapest (BUD).

Trains & Buses

If you prefer ground travel, you can take a train or bus to Iași:

  • From Bucharest: 7-8 hour train ride (comfortable sleeper options available).
  • From Chișinău (Moldova): 4-5 hour bus ride.

Airport Transfers

The airport is 15 minutes from the city center. Options:

  • Taxi: ~50 RON (€10) to the city center. Use Bolt or Uber for fixed prices.
  • Public Transport: Bus #50 (10 RON, ~€2) to the city center.

Local Tips

Currency

Romania uses the Romanian Leu (RON). 1 EUR ≈ 4.9 RON. Credit cards are widely accepted, but carry cash for small vendors.

Language

Romanian is the official language, but English is widely spoken in Iași. Here are a few useful phrases:

  • Bună ziua! – Hello!
  • Mulțumesc! – Thank you!
  • Cât costă? – How much does it cost?

Transport

Iași is walkable, but here are other options:

  • Public Transport: Buses and trams (10 RON per ride).
  • Taxi: Bolt or Uber (fixed prices, no haggling).
  • Walking: The city center is pedestrian-friendly.

Weather

September in Iași is mild (15-25°C). Pack light layers and comfortable shoes for the wedding.
